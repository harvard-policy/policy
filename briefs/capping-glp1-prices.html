Capping the Price of GLP-1s: Quantitative Analysis and Recommendations for Weight Loss Drug Policies in the United States

Team
Economics & Labor team, Harvard IOP Policy Program
Published
Fall 2025
Format
Full report, PDF, open access
Read the full brief (PDF)

Summary

Examines how high demand and limited competition have made GLP-1 drugs such as Ozempic and Wegovy prohibitively expensive, compares coverage abroad, and recommends a federal price cap modeled on the insulin cap alongside reforms to pharmacy benefit managers and FDA approvals.

Recommendations

  1. Implement mandatory federal price caps on GLP-1s based on international pricing standards
  2. Mandate Medicaid coverage for weight management in states with large food-desert populations
  3. Require fixed fees instead of rebates or spread pricing for pharmacy benefit managers, and enforce PBM transparency
  4. Streamline FDA approval of compounded and generic GLP-1 medications
  5. Extend FDA direct-to-consumer advertising rules to social media and influencer promotion
