Charter Schools in the United States: History, Outcomes, and Policy Recommendations

Team
Education Policy team, Harvard IOP Policy Program
Published
Fall 2025
Format
Full report, PDF, open access
Read the full brief (PDF)

Summary

Traces the history, funding and spread of U.S. charter schools, reviews their academic, economic and teacher-labor outcomes through case studies, and recommends steps on teacher quality, equitable outreach, for-profit operators and funding disparities.

Recommendations

  1. Attract better teachers through teacher credential programs
  2. Create more equitable charter schools through better outreach
  3. Regulate and phase out for-profit education management organizations
  4. Eliminate funding disparities
  5. Pay bonuses to teachers whose students score highly on AP exams
