Expanding Access to Study Abroad: History, Outcomes, and Policy Recommendations

Team
Education Policy team, Harvard IOP Policy Program
Published
Summer 2025
Format
Full report, PDF, open access
Read the full brief (PDF)

Summary

Reviews the history and outcomes of study abroad and the racial and socioeconomic gaps in who takes part, drawing on programs such as Gilman, Project GO and Erasmus, and recommends grants, job support and a European-style credit transfer system to widen access.

Recommendations

  1. Improve advertising of study abroad programs
  2. Create more robust grants for study abroad
  3. Provide job support to study abroad participants
  4. Invest in study abroad faculty, including intercultural-competence training
  5. Establish a credit transfer system modeled on Europe's ECTS
