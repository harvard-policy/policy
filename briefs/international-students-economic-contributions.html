The Economic and Societal Contributions of International Students at Harvard and Beyond

Team
Economics & Labor team, Harvard IOP Policy Program
Published
Summer 2025
Format
Full report, PDF, open access
Read the full brief (PDF)

Summary

Documents the contributions of international students to the U.S. economy, research and soft power, from Harvard's programs in eleven states to case studies of other universities, and proposes visa reforms to keep that talent in the country.

Recommendations

  1. Reform F-1 policy, with automatic three-year post-graduation work authorization and no per-country green card caps for advanced-degree holders
  2. Create a startup visa pathway for international graduate founders
  3. Create a tiered security-clearance visa to retain STEM talent
  4. Exempt international graduates from the H-1B cap and streamline the move from F-1 to H-1B
  5. Prevent gentrification through city and university collaboration
