Addressing Persistent Workforce Shortages in Massachusetts Community Health Centers: Recruitment, Retention, and Statewide Investment

Team
Health Policy team, Harvard IOP Policy Program
Published
Summer 2025
Format
Full report, PDF, open access
Read the full brief (PDF)

Summary

Finds that Massachusetts community health centers are the provider type most likely to be short-staffed, and recommends legislative, executive and partner actions on training, Medicaid rates, loan repayment, licensure and support for clinicians.

Recommendations

  1. Amend and enact S.900 to target Medicaid graduate medical education funding to rural and underserved health centers
  2. Enact rate equity for community health centers (S.711/H.1096)
  3. Expand loan repayment for primary care clinicians
  4. Require reimbursement for interpreter services and join the Interstate Medical Licensure Compact
  5. Create practitioner tax credits, an associate physician licensure pathway, housing subsidies and Tri-Share childcare
