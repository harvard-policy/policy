Reducing Maternal Mortality in Texas: Analysis and Recommendations for State Maternal Health Policies

Team
Health Policy team, Harvard IOP Policy Program
Published
Spring 2026
Format
Full report, PDF, open access
Read the full brief (PDF)

Summary

Analyzes the social, structural and health-system drivers of poor maternal health outcomes in Texas, from rural maternity care deserts and disparities facing immigrant and Black mothers to gaps in paid leave and Medicaid, and proposes state policy interventions in each area.

Recommendations

  1. Mandate Medicaid reimbursement for oral language interpreters in hospitals
  2. Expand tele-maternal health infrastructure in maternal care deserts
  3. Shield OB/GYNs from liability when they follow ACOG standards
  4. Implement a Texas paid family leave program
  5. Expand Medicaid reimbursement for doulas and community health workers, with automatic Medicaid enrollment at the point of care
