Venezuela and the Global Oil Market: History, Infrastructure, Geopolitics, and the Prospects for U.S. Reinvestment

Team
Policy Program, Harvard IOP Policy Program
Published
Spring 2026
Format
Full report, PDF, open access
Read the full brief (PDF)

Summary

Traces Venezuela's oil history, the technical demands of its heavy crude and the sector after Nicolás Maduro's capture in January 2026, and concludes that U.S. reinvestment should be phased and conditional, backed by targeted government support.

Recommendations

  1. A per-barrel purchase incentive for Gulf Coast refiners processing Venezuelan heavy crude
  2. A 30% capital-expenditure tax credit for producers, modeled on Section 48C
  3. Political risk insurance and government-backed financing through EXIM and the DFC
  4. Phased investment tied to political conditions: elections, anti-corruption and sanctions-for-reform
